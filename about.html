HOW IT STARTED

Because sometimes you need nowhere else to be.

Solace was created with one simple idea: everyone deserves a peaceful place to slow down and enjoy a good cup of coffee.

We wanted to create a warm coffee house where guests can meet friends, study, work or simply enjoy a quiet moment.

From carefully prepared coffee to our welcoming atmosphere, every detail at Solace is made with our guests in mind.
